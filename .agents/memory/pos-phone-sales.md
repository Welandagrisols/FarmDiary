---
name: POS and phone sales
description: Product requirements for Farm Diary sales and receipts across Sunmi POS devices and phones.
---

Farm Diary must run on Sunmi POS devices as well as phones. Managers must not be mandatory approvers for ordinary sales.

**Why:** The user said manager approval creates a bottleneck and the system will run on both Sunmi devices and phones.

**How to apply:** Design the sale confirmation and receipt flow to work on either device without a manager-only gate. Keep separate controls only where the user has not asked to remove them.
