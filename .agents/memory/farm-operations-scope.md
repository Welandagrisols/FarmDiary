---
name: Farm operations scope
description: User requirements for broad farm activity capture, labour effectiveness, and future livestock and poultry support.
---

Workers need to be able to record any farm operation, including irrigation or watering, weeding, land preparation, harvesting, pest and disease spraying, scouting, and activities that are not already listed. Owners and managers need to track labour effectiveness. Livestock and poultry support is planned for later.

**Why:** The user stated these requirements for this app.

**How to apply:** Keep activity capture open to user-entered operations, support labour effectiveness tracking, and avoid treating crop production as the only future farm domain.
