---
name: Farm account provisioning
description: Account creation policy for the Agrisols Farm Diary app.
---

Farm accounts are created by a farm manager. Farmers sign in with credentials provided by their manager; do not offer public self-service signup on the farmer login screen.

**Why:** The user supplied the running app's sign-in screen as the reference for the workspace version.

**How to apply:** Keep farmer access sign-in-only and preserve manager-controlled account creation.
